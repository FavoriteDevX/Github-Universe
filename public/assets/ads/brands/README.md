# Real brand ads (optional)

Drop an ad image named after the brand key (`.jpg`) here and every billboard of
that brand shows it instead of the generated logo board, for example
`github.jpg`, `google.jpg`, `nvidia.jpg`, `openai.jpg`, `microsoft.jpg`,
`docker.jpg`, `vercel.jpg`. Keys are the Simple Icons slugs (lowercase, no
spaces) or the names listed in ../../logos/README.md.

Ads are copyrighted by their owners: only publish images you have the right to use.
